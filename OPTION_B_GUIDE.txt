============================================================
 OPTION B  -  Train a Custom Toy-Car Model  (summary)
============================================================

GOAL
----
Build a YOLOv11n model that is trained specifically on YOUR
toy cars in YOUR parking layout, then deploy it to the Raspberry
Pi 4 in NCNN format for fast inference.

Higher accuracy than the baseline because the model sees exactly
what it will encounter at runtime.


TIME BUDGET
-----------
  Step 2  Capture photos          20 - 30 min
  Step 3  Auto-pre-label           2 -  5 min
  Step 4  Review labels           30 - 60 min   (most tedious)
  Step 5  Generate data.yaml         5 sec
  Step 6  Fine-tune (GTX 1650 Ti) 25 - 45 min
  Step 7  Export to NCNN              1 min
  TOTAL                          ~ 1.5 - 2.5 hours


WHERE TO RUN EVERYTHING
-----------------------
On the PC (Windows), in this folder:
   D:\Work and college\college\projects\embded system\PC_SCRIPTS

Open CMD here, then run scripts with:    py -3.13 <script>.py


=====================================================================
 STEP 2 - CAPTURE TRAINING PHOTOS
=====================================================================
Command:
   py -3.13 2_capture_training_data.py

Webcam window opens. Captures save to data/raw_images/

Controls in the window:
   SPACE  capture one photo
   A      toggle AUTO mode (1 photo every 1.5 s)
   D      delete last captured photo
   Q      quit

Coverage to aim for (~300 photos total):
   * Every toy car you own, individually
   * Multiple cars together (2, 3, 4)
   * Cars INSIDE parking spots
   * Cars OUTSIDE parking spots (on the road area)
   * Cars half-in / half-out of spots
   * Empty parking lot  (at least 30 photos with no cars)
   * Different rotations / angles of cars
   * Different lighting (room light on, off, sunny, evening)
   * Some photos with distractors (your hand, a coin, etc.)
   * From the SAME camera angle / height you'll use on RPi4

Targets:
   minimum     = 150 photos
   recommended = 300 photos
   excellent   = 500 photos

Practical tips:
   * Mount the camera on a tripod or stand. Don't hand-hold.
   * Build the final parking layout BEFORE capturing.
   * Use AUTO mode + slowly slide cars around for ~3 minutes
     (gives ~120 photos with almost no effort).
   * Press D immediately if a photo is blurry / has you in it.


=====================================================================
 STEP 3 - AUTO PRE-LABEL
=====================================================================
Command:
   py -3.13 3_auto_label.py

What it does:
   * Loads YOLOv8s (the "small" COCO model, ~22 MB).
   * Runs detection on every photo at low confidence (0.20).
   * Maps detected {car, motorcycle, bus, truck} -> class 0 (vehicle).
   * Splits photos 85% train / 15% valid.
   * Writes YOLO .txt label files next to each image.

Output:
   data/toy_dataset/
      train/images/   train/labels/
      valid/images/   valid/labels/

Watch the console output. If it says
   "Images with at least 1 detection : X / 300"
and X is less than ~50% of total, your toy cars look unusual to
COCO. That's fine - you'll just do more manual work in Step 4.


=====================================================================
 STEP 4 - REVIEW & CORRECT LABELS  (labelImg)   <-- most tedious
=====================================================================
Command:
   py -3.13 4_review_labels.py

This opens labelImg twice (train, then valid).

FIRST-TIME labelImg setup (do this once):
   1. Left sidebar -> format dropdown -> select  YOLO   (NOT Pascal VOC).
   2. Menu  View -> Auto Save Mode  (so you don't lose work).
   3. Menu  View -> Auto Use Default Label  -> set to "vehicle".

Per-image workflow:
   * Press  D  for next image.
   * Box around something that's NOT a toy car?  click it, press Delete.
   * Toy car with NO box?  press W, drag a tight box, label = vehicle.
   * Box too loose?  click and drag corners to tighten.

Speed tips:
   * Most images need only 5-10 seconds of review.
   * If auto-labels are already perfect, just hold D to fly past.
   * Use Ctrl+D to duplicate the previous bbox when cars don't move.

Common labelImg controls:
   W         draw new bbox
   D         next image
   A         previous image
   Del       delete selected bbox
   Ctrl+S    save  (or just enable Auto Save)


=====================================================================
 STEP 5 - GENERATE data.yaml
=====================================================================
Command:
   py -3.13 5_prepare_dataset.py

Takes 1 second. Just creates data/data.yaml with absolute paths.

If it says "0 labeled images in train" -> you forgot to save in
labelImg. Go back to step 4.


=====================================================================
 STEP 6 - FINE-TUNE  YOLOv11n
=====================================================================
Command:
   py -3.13 6_finetune_model.py

Two-stage training (NVIDIA GPU is auto-detected and used):

  STAGE 1  -  20 epochs, ~5 min
     * Backbone frozen, head only trains.
     * Prevents overfitting on the small dataset.

  STAGE 2  -  80 epochs, ~20-35 min
     * Full unfreeze, lower LR (0.0005), cosine schedule.
     * Strong augmentation: rotation, color jitter, mosaic, mixup.
     * Augmentation is critical for small datasets.

Input size = 256 (matches the RPi4 deployment size).

Live console shows:
   Epoch    box_loss   cls_loss   dfl_loss   mAP50
   10/80    1.21       0.42       1.05       0.78
   20/80    0.98       0.31       0.92       0.86
   ...

Final mAP guide:
   0.95 +        excellent, model nails it
   0.85 - 0.95   good, production-ready
   0.70 - 0.85   ok, but capture more varied photos
   < 0.70        need more / better labeled data

If you see CUDA out-of-memory:
   Open 6_finetune_model.py and change BATCH_SIZE = 16 to 8 or 4.

Outputs in:
   runs/parking/stage2_full/weights/best.pt    <-- your model
   runs/parking/stage2_full/results.png        <-- loss curves
   runs/parking/stage2_full/val_batch0_pred.jpg<-- predictions on
                                                    validation images
                                                    (LOOK AT THIS!)


=====================================================================
 STEP 7 - EXPORT TO NCNN
=====================================================================
Command:
   py -3.13 7_export_model.py

Takes ~1 minute. Exports best.pt -> NCNN at 256x256 and copies
the result automatically to:

   RPI4_SCRIPTS/models/yolo11n_ncnn_model/

After this, the RPI4_SCRIPTS folder is READY to flash to USB
and copy to the Raspberry Pi.


=====================================================================
 STEP 8 - SANITY CHECK ON PC  (recommended)
=====================================================================
Command:
   py -3.13 8_test_model_pc.py
or for a raw detection-only view:
   py -3.13 test_detection_only.py

Place toy cars in front of the camera. You should see:
   * Tight green boxes on every toy car.
   * Confidence > 0.7 on most boxes.
   * No boxes on hands, mouse, keyboard, etc.

If accuracy is poor, DON'T deploy yet:
   * Add more photos of the failing cases (step 2).
   * Re-run steps 3 -> 7.


=====================================================================
 ITERATING LATER (adding new toy cars / new layout)
=====================================================================
1. py -3.13 2_capture_training_data.py
   -> appends to data/raw_images/, doesn't delete old photos.
2. py -3.13 3_auto_label.py
   -> rebuilds train/valid splits, re-labels everything.
3. py -3.13 4_review_labels.py
   -> only review the new images (old labels are kept).
4. Continue 5 -> 7.

After the first time, the iteration loop is ~30 minutes total.


=====================================================================
 COMMON PITFALLS
=====================================================================
   * All photos taken from same angle           -> change height,
                                                    re-aim slightly.
   * All photos in same lighting                -> turn lights on/off,
                                                    different times.
   * No empty-lot photos                        -> capture at least 30.
   * Inconsistent labeling                      -> pick a rule and
                                                    stick to it.
   * Trained at wrong imgsz                     -> step 6 must say
                                                    imgsz=256 to match
                                                    step 7 export and
                                                    RPi4 deployment.


=====================================================================
 DEPLOYMENT TO RPi4 AFTER OPTION B
=====================================================================
1. Copy the updated  RPI4_SCRIPTS/  folder to a USB stick.
2. On the Pi:
      cp -r /media/$USER/<USB>/RPI4_SCRIPTS ~/parking
      cd ~/parking
      source parking_env/bin/activate

3. (Optional) Test raw detection with no parking logic:
      python3 test_detection_only.py

4. Define parking slots once:
      python3 annotate_slots.py

5. Run the full system:
      python3 parking_system.py
